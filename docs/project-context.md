# North

## Mission

North helps people build their identity into who they want to become by creating consistency in the smallest pieces of their daily life.

The goal is not simply to complete tasks or maintain streaks.

The goal is to help users become the person they want to be through repeated action over time.

## Target Audience

People who want to intentionally improve their lives and become more aligned with the person they aspire to be.

This includes:

* Individuals pursuing personal growth
* Fitness enthusiasts
* Parents
* Professionals
* Students
* Anyone seeking greater consistency and self-discipline

The founder is also a primary user of the application.

## Product Principles

* Free forever
* No freemium model
* No feature gating
* Cross-platform
* Mobile-first
* Offline-first
* Fast and lightweight
* Flexible habit structures
* Consistency over perfection
* Optional identity-based habit organization
* User-owned data
* Import-friendly
* Export-friendly

## Competitive Positioning

North aims to provide an excellent free habit-tracking experience, especially on iOS where quality free options are limited.

Compared to competitors:

* Avoids heavy RPG mechanics
* Cross-platform rather than Android-only
* Supports a wider range of habit structures
* Emphasizes consistency and identity formation
* Prioritizes user ownership of data

## Long-Term Vision

North becomes a trusted system for helping users intentionally build the life they want through repeated action.

The application should feel empowering, lightweight, sustainable, and accessible to everyone.

## Monetization Philosophy

North is intended to remain free forever.

The product should not be designed around subscriptions, habit limits, feature gates, or paywalls.

Architectural decisions should prioritize sustainability while preserving a complete free experience.
